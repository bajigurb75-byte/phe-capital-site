What we look for

We are generalists by mandate and specific by taste. Four things decide almost every check we write.

Something already running

A testnet, a contract, a prototype, a community that showed up before you paid for it. If you are pre-code, show us a credible path to shipping inside 90 days.

Founders who explain the mechanism

Not the market size. We want the person who can walk us through how the thing actually settles, clears, or earns, and where it breaks under load.

Value that accrues somewhere real

Fees, spread, float, or a token with a job beyond governance. We would rather back honest revenue than an emissions schedule dressed up as demand.

A reason it works now

Most crypto ideas failed once already. Tell us what changed — the cost, the infrastructure, the regulation, the behaviour — that makes this the attempt that lands.

Where we invest

We spend most of our time in four areas. The fifth line is not a formality — some of our best positions arrived unannounced.

Infrastructure

Execution layers, data availability, sequencing, bridges, restaking, and the unglamorous plumbing underneath all of it.

DeFi

Exchanges, lending, perps and derivatives, stablecoins, yield primitives, and real-world assets brought on-chain properly.

Trading and liquidity

Market structure, order flow, MEV, on-chain execution, and the tooling desks actually use during a volatile week.

Consumer and payments

Wallets, on-ramps, stablecoin rails, social and gaming economies — anywhere crypto disappears behind a product that works.

And whatever this list does not cover yet

If your project is genuinely new and does not fit a category above, send it anyway. We read everything, and the interesting things rarely arrive pre-labelled.

After the check

A $25k check and a $250k check get the same phone number. Here is what we are actually useful for.

Liquidity and listings

Introductions to market makers, exchanges, and desks — plus a frank read on which of those conversations are worth your time at your stage.

Supply, unlocks, incentives, and launch mechanics reviewed before you commit to them publicly, while the numbers can still change.
